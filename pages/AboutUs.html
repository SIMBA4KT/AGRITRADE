Our Story

Why we started AgriTrade

AgriTrade began with a simple observation: local farmers were working harder than ever but seeing less profit, while consumers were paying more for produce that aren't always fresh.

By removing the middleman, we created a digital bridge. Today, we help hundreds of farmers list their harvests directly, ensuring fair pay for them and farm-to-table freshness for you.
